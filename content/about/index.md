Hello! I'm Anu Agarwal, from Champaign, Illinois.

By day, I decode intelligence—building smarter AI models and optimizing deep learning frameworks. 

I am a Master's student in Computer Science at the University of Illinois, Urbana-Champaign, specializing in Generative AI, Machine Learning, and Computer Vision. As a Graduate Research Assistant at NCSA, I apply computer vision and machine learning to predict animal behavior. 

Prior to starting grad school, I've worked extensively in the industry, developing financial models at Goldman Sachs, optimizing data systems at Confluent, and reconstruction indoor scenes in 3D at Samsung Semiconductors. My research spans LLM optimization, generative AI, and real-world AI applications. 

But when I’m not training models, you’ll find me painting vibrant canvases or singing my heart out—because creativity isn’t just for code. 🎨🎶
